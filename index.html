Our idea

Good ideas start on paper.

THE INKCIDENT began as a student startup concept: a chance to rethink the small things we reach for every day. We're sketching familiar stationery with care, asking questions about materials and how things might be used for longer.

It's early, and the details are still taking shape. For now, this is a place to share the direction and keep the thinking open.
